# Hqq_v2 BigTMD check

## Scope

This folder compares the accepted finite MS-bar `Hqq_v2` hats in
`../s08_result.wl` with the pinned BigTMD channel-2 implementation.  It is a
read-only downstream benchmark: it does not alter or rederive any accepted
Hqq_v2 artifact.

The authoritative physics reference is
`../../Large_Transverse_Momentum_in_Semi-Inclusive_Deeply_Inelastic_Scattering_Beyond_Lowest_Order.pdf`.
Live execution status is recorded only in `../../progress.md` with the exact
tag `[Hqq_v2, people or agents working on other channels should ignore]`.

  `../../Hqqprime/bigTMD_check/BigTMD_reference`, commit
  `6e97635d21a63b7975b2e7f5891edc0c35c4dc0c`, with `sidis.py` SHA-256
  `150a4b66ce25c41178a51ef54989dc5a83d7a272678e1d4f95237ddb9758785d`.
- Hqq is BigTMD channel 2.  Cases A, B, and C are combined with the exact
  channel-2 luminosity weights produced by the pinned driver's
  `get_parton_lum` for one fixed incoming/fragmenting up-type flavor and four
  active flavors.  The local side uses the matching exact charge assignment
  and tool-derived up/down multiplicities.
- Both sides use `EL=1`, `g_s=1`, `ScaleMu=mu=Q`, `SU(3)`, and `Nf=4`.
- The local hats already contain the `zeta -> s23` Jacobian.  The matching
  driver Jacobian is applied only to BigTMD's partonic functions.  The common
  remaining luminosity/test factor, PDFs, FFs, and outer convolution are not
  applied to either side.
- Four coefficient-level distribution observables are compared for each hat:
  `Delta`, `Plus0`, `Plus1`, and `Ordinary`.  `Plus0` and `Plus1` are the
  coefficients of `HqqV2BoundedPlus[0,s23,B]` and
  `HqqV2BoundedPlus[1,s23,B]`.  On the BigTMD side they are generated by the
  endpoint values of `plus1B` and `plus2B`.  `Ordinary` includes `regular`
  plus the endpoint-subtracted ordinary remainders of both plus functions,
  exactly as selected by `sidis.py`.
- The generated BigTMD endpoint functions are evaluated with the positive
  `zero` regulator AST-extracted from the pinned driver (`1e-07` in this
  snapshot), exactly matching the driver's delta and plus endpoint calls.
- Every local substitution remains exact through rational benchmark
  insertion.  S01 uses the pinned HyperIntica MPL/MZV reductions and the
  installed FeynHelpers/Package-X numerical evaluator, then records the real
  projection of the already accepted S06 Hermitian expression together with
  its pre-projection branch-imaginary diagnostic.  BigTMD's decimal Python
  expressions are executed exactly as shipped.
- Signed differences are always `BigTMD - local`.  A mismatch is a valid
  completed outcome and must be reported without changing either input.

## Stage contracts

0. `s00_prepare_local_fhats.wl` runs with FeynCalc add-ons disabled,
   hash-validates the accepted Hqq_v2 S08 result and both exact terminal-hat
   identities in their original symbol context, and atomically writes the
   textual bridge artifact `s00_prepared_local_fhats.wl`.
1. `s01_export_local_fhat_benchmarks.wl` loads FeynHelpers/Package-X and the
   pinned retained HyperIntica backend before reading the S00 textual bridge,
   validates its embedded accepted provenance and reference identities,
   extracts the two bounded-plus coefficients symbolically, validates three
   rational interior benchmarks, and atomically writes
   `local_fhat_benchmarks.json`.
2. `s02_compare_bigtmd_fhat.py` hash-validates S01 and the pinned channel-2
   modules, AST-extracts the driver's luminosity definitions and endpoint
   regulator, evaluates the driver-defined A/B/C combination for all four
   observables, constructs `F1Hat` and `F2Hat` from `Pg` and `Ppp`, and
   atomically writes `bigtmd_fhat_benchmarks.json`,
   `bigtmd_minus_local.json`, and `bigtmd_minus_local.md`.

All stages refuse pre-existing output or temporary files.  Their production
logs are `s00_prepare_local_fhats.log`,
`s01_export_local_fhat_benchmarks.log`, and
`s02_compare_bigtmd_fhat.log`.  The S00 bridge exists only to preserve the
accepted hash context while letting S01 bind special-function heads to their
tools; no extra runner, reconstruction, or post-production validator is part
of this check.
